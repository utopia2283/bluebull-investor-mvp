問題 · The problem

One bureau. 一個擁有者。
No plan B.

Hong Kong’s personal credit infrastructure still runs on 1980s patching logic, owned by a single US-domiciled bureau that structurally cannot route cross-border data. The 2025 HKMA dual-CRA mandate doubled compliance load on every retail bank — and the previous third-seat entrant collapsed under manual burn-rate, leaving a wasting asset on the table.

01 · SINGLE POINT OF FAILURE
1 bureau

One bureau serves 5.5M Hong Kong consumers. No redundancy, no competitive benchmark, no public audit.

全港 550 万人共用同一份信用底層。
02 · COMPLIANCE OVERLOAD
+80% cost

The 2025 dual-CRA HKMA directive doubled manual review work. Banks need automation, not another license to display.

2025 金管局雙 CRA 強制指令後、營運成本類軸升。
03 · DEADLOCK AT THE BORDER
0 GBA flow

US-owned architecture cannot legally move personal credit data across the Greater Bay Area border. A trillion-HKD corridor sits unfunded.

美資架構處理不了跨境數據、错失整個大灣區。
